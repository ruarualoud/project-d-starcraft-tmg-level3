由createOfficialReleaseRosterStateV2实例化合法Zagara’s Swarm与选定Creep卡军表。Characteristic Family编译Squadron／Metabolic Boost，Melee Family编译IMPACT1／5+与Claws。Root3.95只对所选新定义登记组合及依赖；原Root分支保留。
