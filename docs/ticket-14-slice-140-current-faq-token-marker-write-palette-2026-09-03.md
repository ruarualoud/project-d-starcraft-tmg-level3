## Outcome

The shared BattleWorkbench now exposes a Rules-bound Token/Marker palette and
one integrated battle write sheet. The client contract pins the current FAQ
aggregate, catalogue, runtime, graph and Token/Marker contract hashes. Its
Token/Marker denominator contains 12 FAQ entries and 27 FAQ atoms while
retaining the Ticket 11 evidence counts of 69 directly named atoms and 11
generic primitives. These sets overlap and are deliberately not added together
or presented as a count of clickable actions.

Only the viewer's exact, revision-bound LegalSpace determines what is currently
clickable. The classifier covers finite actions and parameter domains and
projects verb, type, source, controller, duration, stack/unique policy,
trigger, cleanup timing, legal domains, geometry and related FAQ atoms. The
declared lifecycle is create/place/move/consume/remove. An entry missing the
metadata required to describe its Rules-owned mutation remains visible under
unsupported diagnostics and cannot reach Preview.

## Version boundary

Current FAQ, historical pre-FAQ and invalid/mixed bindings are separate:

- an exact current FAQ binding may label and issue current-rules proposals;
- an exact Ticket 11 binding remains visible as frozen historical rules for
  display/Replay, with mutation disabled;
- a mixed, unknown or legacy-compatibility binding quarantines the palette and
  disables every proposal button.

The current FAQ graph has 567 nodes and 1,308 edges and content-addresses the
frozen Ticket 11 graph with 12,292 nodes and 33,644 edges rather than copying
it. Old rule display and Replay remain retained. Slice 140 does not claim that
the development room fixture is a production current-FAQ room; production room
truth remains false until the remaining Ticket 14 integration gates close.

## Integrated write sheet

Damage, shields, casualties, status, deployment, score and Token/Marker are
seven views over the same LegalSpace. There is no direct number editor and no
client mutation API. A supported write follows:

`LegalSpace → authoritative Preview → explicit human confirmation → Apply → Receipt/Replay`

Expo and Battle Lab mount the same palette. A finite entry requests Preview by
its server action key; a parameter domain opens the existing Actions editor.
The UI disables quarantined and unsupported entries instead of silently
falling back to older or generic behavior.
